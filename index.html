03 / WHY CORSARYN

The procedure ends.
Recovery doesn't.

Regional anesthesia can send a patient home while a nerve block is still working. The transition when it wears off is time-sensitive, personal, and difficult to see through a routine discharge process.

Explore Corsaryn ↗
01

We know the clinical context.

Andrei's research and hands-on pain clinic experience shape the questions Corsaryn asks and the workflows it is designed to support.

02

We know how to build systems.

Rajaram and Manit bring engineering, clinical AI research, and device prototyping to the practical work of making those workflows usable.

03

We are building for the handoff.

Our engineering demonstration organizes short recovery episodes, structured check-ins, and clinician review. The clinical impact still needs prospective evaluation.
